## 1. v2 taker-PnL sign — **RESOLVED (no bug)**

**Scare:** the complete v2 aggregate (`scripts/28`, `docs/v2_platform.json`) came
out with total taker PnL **+$21.3M** (takers *winning*), the opposite sign to v1
(`−$86M`, takers losing). I initially suspected a BUY/SELL or taker/maker swap,
because the size-weighted calibration sign-check (gap **+0.0007** ≈ 0) cannot by
itself catch a side flip (a flip leaves calibration ≈ 0 in an efficient market).

**Decisive proof.** On one clean subrange (blocks 88.08M–88.30M) I compared
scripts/28's convention against the unambiguous ground truth — the
`is_taker_order=TRUE` records, which carry the taker order's **own** side (taker
wallet in the `maker` field, taker field = exchange):

| convention | records | taker PnL | calib gap |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| **[GT]** taker order (`is_tk=TRUE`), own side | 8,365,279 | **+7,494,346** | **+0.0075** |
| **[s28]** maker-fill, `opposite(side)` = aggressor | 14,269,814 | **+7,494,346** | **+0.0075** |
| [LP] maker-fill, `side` as-is = maker/LP | 14,269,814 | −7,494,346 | −0.0075 |

[GT] and [s28] agree **to the dollar and the basis point** — they are the same
trades seen from complementary sides, so `opposite(maker-fill side)` **is** the
taker's own side. scripts/28's sign is **correct**. The false premise behind the
scare was "takers must lose": on the v2 tape the aggressor is mildly *informed* /
the LP is *adversely selected*, so the taker calibration gap is slightly **positive**
— a coherent economic story, not a convention flip.

## 2. v1 ↔ v2 "taker" is the same economic party — **RESOLVED**

- **v1** (`scripts/27_platform_wide.py`): uses the archive's `taker_direction`
  **directly** (aggressor's own side); PnL `BUY:(won−price)·sh / SELL:(price−won)·sh`.
- **v2** (`scripts/28_v2_platform.py`): `taker_side = opposite(maker-fill side)`
  (proven = aggressor's own side above), taker = the `taker` field; **identical**
  PnL formula. Winner test: v1 `outcome_label==winning_outcome_label`, v2
  `outcome_index==winning_outcome_index` — equivalent.

Both measure the aggressor with the aggressor's own direction. The v1 (−$86M,
small negative gap) vs v2 (+$21.3M, gap +0.0007 ≈ 0) difference is a **real
cross-era difference** (v1 = 2022–2026 event/political markets; v2 = 2026-06+,
~69% crypto hourly up/down), not a sign artifact. Both eras are near-efficient
(tiny calibration gaps); the honest headline is "v2 takers ≈ calibrated, v1
takers carry a small negative edge."

**Comparison caveats to state wherever v1/v2 are compared:** different time spans
(v1 ~746M fills over years vs v2 ~436M linked fills over ~3 months) — normalize
per-fill/per-notional, not raw totals; and the v2 2.19% chunk-overlap inflates v2
PnL magnitude / Gini / leaderboards (calibration ratios are exact).

Two wrong turns before the right answer (both caught in this audit; recorded for honesty):
1. First reported **+$21.3M "takers win"** — artifact of one-sided undedup inflation.
2. Then computed **NET ≈ gross** because scripts/28 subtracted **maker-fill `fee_raw`**,
   which is ≈0. **The taker fee is charged once per taker order and lives on the
   `is_taker_order=TRUE` records** (verified: ~0.9–1.6% of notional there vs ~0.001% on
   maker-fill rows), where the taker wallet is the `maker` field.

**Corrected, deduped, resolved-scope figures (2026-04 → 2026-08):**

| metric | value |
|---|--:|
| deduped linked maker-fills | 426,807,969 |
| GROSS taker PnL (spread/calibration only) | **+$20.03M** (gap +0.0007 ≈ 0) |
| total taker fee (`is_tk` records, 1.167% of $12.81B notional) | **$149.53M** |
| **NET taker PnL** | **−$129.50M** |

dominates, so NET is uniformly negative.

**Corrected v1↔v2 conclusion:** **takers lose in BOTH eras — no reversal.** v1
(~fee-free): −$86M, lost to the spread. v2: ≈break-even on the spread (gap ≈ 0) but
